# From Newtonian prediction to quantum description

## Authoring contract

This prospective plan responds to the user's rejection of a catalog of disconnected short episodes. Produce one explanatory work, not a playlist or concatenation. Central question: why did successful laws of motion and classical pictures of matter cease to supply a sufficient account, and what did quantum theory change? The mainline must answer that question without requiring a detour. The reader knows ordinary motion and that light and matter interact; introduce additional terms through a concrete problem.

Preserve observation, model, deduction, history, and interpretation as distinct. Do not imply that every discovery happened in the teaching order, that classical mechanics simply became false, that one experiment proved all quantum theory, or that particles were literally photographed following the authored paths. Reuse scoped claim IDs instead of copying their definitions; new bridge/history assertions require exact consulted sources. All review, rights, source-revision, learner, and publication states remain unresolved until actually checked.
