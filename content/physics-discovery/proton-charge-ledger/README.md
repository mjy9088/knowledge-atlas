# Proton charge-ledger episode

## Explanatory contract

This candidate answers one question: why does the proton label `uud` not mean that exactly three classical particles exhaust every instantaneous proton state? It treats `uud` as the minimal valence-flavor ledger, works `2/3 + 2/3 − 1/3 = 1`, and then changes the state by adding a sea `uū` pair and a gluon. The unchanged electric charge demonstrates why one additive quantity cannot determine constituent count.

Gluons are described as **electrically neutral**, not simply neutral: they carry color charge and interact strongly. The episode does not introduce Bjorken `x` or equate it with a PDF momentum-fraction variable beyond the naive leading-order parton picture. It makes no claim that the photon experiment alone proves confinement, exact constituent count, equal one-third momenta, or a three-bead image.

## Passages actually read

- **Michael Riordan, SLAC-PUB-5724, “The Discovery of Quarks” (1992):** section “The Prediction of Quarks,” paragraph beginning “In seeking a deeper explanation,” states that baryons are built from three quarks, identifies the proton as `uud`, and gives `u` charge `+2/3e` and `d`/`s` charge `−1/3e`. This retrospective article is not treated as the primary 1969 DIS measurement report.
- **PDG 2025 Quark Model review:** §15.2 printed p. 3, Table 15.1, gives `u` and `d` electric charges. §15.5 printed p. 13 says that baryons in the most general case contain three quarks plus any number of quark–antiquark pairs. §15.5.1 printed p. 18 calls the three effective quarks in the standard nonrelativistic model valence quarks. The opening warning that “quark model” has multiple levels motivates the non-bead limit.
- **CERN CMS, 25 October 2024:** the opening paragraph explicitly contrasts the common `uud` proton and `udd` neutron picture with the complex sea of quark–antiquark pairs and gluons. It supports the scope correction, not the historical charge discovery.
- **CERN ATLAS, displayed 12 January 2022:** the opening paragraphs define PDFs through momentum fractions carried by quarks and gluons and say lepton–proton measurements reveal a sea of other quark–antiquark pairs.
- **PDG 2025 QCD review:** §9.1 printed pp. 1–2 states that gluon fields transform in the adjoint representation of SU(3) color and that free quarks or gluons are not observed; §9.1.2 printed pp. 3–4 relates free-quark absence to long-distance confinement. These passages delimit the explanation; they do not make the charge sum a confinement proof.
- **Nobel Prize 1990 press release:** the opening and “The first traces of quarks” distinguish electrically charged hard grains identified as quarks from electrically neutral gluonic components. This supplies the electric qualifier; color charge is separately supported by the PDG QCD passage.
