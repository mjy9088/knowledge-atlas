# Quark-evidence figure design contract

## 1. Scope and visual direction

This contract extends the shared paper-and-ink reader with original static schematics that expose one epistemic chain: **single-event variables → ensemble cross section → processed comparison → assumption-dependent extraction → model inference**. The figures are qualitative role graphics, not source-figure adaptations, historical plots, reconstructed measurements, uncertainty bands, fitted curves, detector photographs, event displays, or arbitrary numerical data.

The ten stable scene IDs, three claim IDs, exact sentence coverage, `needs-content-review` article state, `planned` 156-second timing, and `review-required` quality state remain unchanged. No topic adapter or quiz is added; the complete comparisons are worked exposition.

## 2. Existing design system and accessibility

- Reuse the shared reader tokens: paper `#f6f8f0`, white surface, ink `#293f35`, structure green `#365c42`, observation amber `#a56d30`, muted ink `#526b58`, and line `#d5dfcc`. Use borders-only depth, 4-unit spacing, 12–16-unit radii, and the inherited Korean font stack.
- Every SVG has a Korean title, description, visible epistemic labels, nonempty alternative text, and a caption that says whether the graphic is a qualitative schematic or authored worked relation.
- Every visible SVG label is at least 24 units in a `600 × 380` view box. Labels remain few, use reflow rather than shrinking, and stay outside curves/arrows. Captions remain at least 13 CSS pixels in every profile.
- Every SVG marker uses `markerUnits="userSpaceOnUse"` and a marker box no larger than 12 units in either dimension. Arrow endpoints stay clear of labels and panels.
- Topic prose, cards, and captions use Korean `word-break: keep-all`, `overflow-wrap: anywhere`, and `text-wrap: pretty`. Mathematical equalities stay inline-safe rather than leaving a detached minus sign or relation symbol.
- Color is redundant with labels, grouping, line style, and arrow direction. No interpretation depends on color alone.

## 3. Representation primitives

- **Epistemic pipeline:** four bordered stages labeled `측정`, `처리`, `조건부 패턴`, and `해석`, joined by nondegenerate forward arrows. It never implies that processing or interpretation was automatic or unique.
- **Event and ensemble sequence:** one collision supplies measured `E′` and `θ` given beam energy `E`; only an ensemble binned in those variables supplies a differential cross section. In the proton-rest laboratory frame and `c=1`, define positive `Q² = −qμqμ ≈ 4EE′ sin²(θ/2)`, `ν = E − E′`, and hadronic invariant mass `W` by `W² = M² + 2Mν − Q²`, where `M` is the proton mass. `W` is never confused with structure functions `W₁` and `W₂`; energies use GeV and squared invariants use GeV².
- **Processing ledger and fixed-`W` comparison:** at matched `E`, `E′`, and `θ`, the measured differential cross section divided by the Mott cross section is `W₂ + 2W₁ tan²(θ/2)`, not a unique `W₂`. The primary paper's residual comparison varies positive `Q²` while holding hadronic invariant mass `W` fixed; only then is the deep-continuum dependence described as weaker than elastic/resonance behavior. No mixed-invariant slope comparison is allowed.
- **Conditional-dependence map:** the small-angle spectra do not separately determine function-valued `W₁` and `W₂`. `R = σL/σT` enters through model-extraction extremes, not a measured uncertainty band: `R=0` is transverse dominance, while `R→∞` is longitudinal dominance.
- **Two-row conditional contrast:** only the transverse-dominance extraction (`R=0`) supports the abstract's conservative statement that `νW₂` is a function of `ω = 2Mν/Q²` within experimental errors for `Q² > 1 GeV²` and `ω > 4`. Under `R→∞`, the extracted `νW₂` does not follow a universal curve and increases with `Q²` at fixed `ω`. Rows use no points, coefficients, or invented uncertainty envelope.
- **Inference ledger:** an extended coherent charge distribution carries form-factor suppression at increasing resolution; incoherent scattering from an unresolved independent charged center permits weak residual dependence/scaling in the parton approximation. The center is one illustrative generic mark labeled “number not specified,” never three constituent beads. Nobel's broad retrospective supports later identification with the quark model; this reader does not derive a complete quark identity from the proton pattern. Neutral gluons are not direct photon charge centers and enter through separate **`Q²`-dependent scaling violations** and later evidence.

## 4. Responsive and state contract

Figures scale at intrinsic aspect ratio inside the shared scene slot with no clipped labels or internal scrolling. Desktop/FHD and `390 × 844` narrow layouts preserve complete prose, figure, caption, and navigation. Topic styles only enlarge compact cards/copy and preserve Korean wrapping; they do not alter shared reader chrome.

These scenes have no scientific input or hidden topic state. Shared reader `getState`, validated `setState`, `reset`, direct scene navigation, and restoration remain the only state contract. Invalid state must be rejected atomically. Browser execution belongs to the lead.
