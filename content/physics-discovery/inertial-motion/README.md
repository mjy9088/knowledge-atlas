# Inertial motion episode

## Explanatory contract

The reader begins with the misleading everyday pattern “moving objects stop,” changes surface resistance while explicitly holding the object, initial speed, level, observation duration, and drag conditions fixed, and uses the changed outcome to isolate friction as a cause of deceleration. Only then does it state the first-law conditional. The episode treats a perfectly resistance-free surface as an ideal limit, not an observed apparatus, and makes the reference-frame condition visible before the transfer question.

The cards compare conditions and forces; they are not pictures of invisible mechanisms. `quality.json` plans 158 seconds across all six scenes. The final car example starts from balanced horizontal and vertical forces in an approximately inertial frame; it does not infer force balance from constant speed alone.
