# Atomic evidence episode

## Explanatory contract

This episode does not use Dalton’s postulates as proof that atoms exist and does not turn a model illustration into an observation. It begins with the epistemic question, identifies the visible colloidal particle as a proxy, moves through a quantitative molecular prediction, and then emphasizes convergence among distinct estimates of Avogadro’s number. The transfer question asks what extra controls would turn an irregular dust path into evidence rather than an anecdote.

`quality.json` plans 164 seconds. The textual cards separate observation, model, measured quantity, and inference. No Brownian trajectory is presented as measured project data.

The scoped visual contract and retrospective proposed seven-scene pre-production plan are in [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md). It requires original static representations for the visible-proxy boundary, one worked translational bridge from measured mean-square displacement to `Nₐ`, and the three reported estimates on one historical-unit axis. `question`, `uncertainty`, `limit`, and `transfer` retain cards where a new figure would add no mechanism or would imply unsupported uncertainty, comparability, or assessment.
