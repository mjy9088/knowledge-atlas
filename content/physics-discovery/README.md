# Physics discovery short series

## Editorial purpose

This local-review series asks how a physical claim became warranted: what was changed or measured, what pattern appeared, what inference the pattern supported, and what remained unresolved. Six Korean articles move from ordinary motion through atomic and nuclear evidence to quantized spectra without presenting history as a predetermined march toward current theory. Each page uses the shared static reader, original text cards, and no interactive adapter. The series ID is `series:physics-discovery`; `DISCOVERY_ENTRIES` from `catalog.ts` is included in the canonical content catalog, the canonical references live in `sources/registry.json`, and the corresponding proposed concepts live in `knowledge/concepts.json`.

The intended reader knows everyday motion and basic ideas of charge but need not know calculus, statistical mechanics, or quantum theory. Vocabulary appears only when it does explanatory work. A later episode may connect nuclei to protons, neutrons, quarks, quantum theory, or relativity, but these articles do not pretend that the three selected experiments established those later structures.
