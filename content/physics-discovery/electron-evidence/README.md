# Electron evidence episode

This episode separates the crossed-field control from the quantity inferred. It shows both balances, `|q|E=|q|vB` and `|q|vB=mv²/r`, and keeps charge sign separate from charge magnitude. Thomson's apparatus yields charge-to-mass ratio, not electron mass by itself. Repeatability is limited to the gases and electron-release methods supported by the consulted source.

The episode consults `source:openstax-college-physics-electrons-nuclei` at §30.2 around equations 30.1–30.3 and the subsequent Thomson/Millikan discussion. The radial step `|q|vB=mv²/r` is an independently written deduction from the sourced magnetic-force expression and centripetal acceleration; equations 30.4–30.5 concern electric deflection and are not cited for that step.
