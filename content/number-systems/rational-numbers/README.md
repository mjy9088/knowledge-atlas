# Rational numbers

The episode treats fractions as representations, not as unique names. It introduces `(a,b)` with `b≠0`, the cross-product equivalence test, and the midpoint proof of order density. Division by zero is excluded explicitly. A skippable next-question scene prevents the common overreach from “between any two rationals lies another” to “rationals are complete.”

The completeness scene is only a scoped gap question: density does not say that every real-line boundary is rational. The dedicated irrational-number and real-completeness episodes now own the `√2` proof and rational non-completeness argument; this episode explicitly does not claim to have proved them.
