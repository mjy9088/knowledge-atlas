# Possibility exploration adapter

[possibility-lab.ts](possibility-lab.ts) mounts the registered binary-relation, Monty Hall and family-observation experiments. [The pure model](../../../../packages/models/possibility-lab.ts) owns validation, counter-based draws, trial prefixes and absolute `labFrameAt(state, ms)`. [Finite observations](../../../../packages/models/finite-observations.ts) owns exact reference probabilities. DOM animation is a projection, never an authority for the model.

## State and scenes

The adapter implements the shared topic interaction contract: cloned `getState`, atomic validated `setState`, `reset`, absolute `seek`, `inspect`, `suggest` and `setActive`. State contains the topic, uint32 seed, attempted count (0–10,000), branching stage (0–3), four-bit directed relation, initial door, observation mode and reveal flag. Every random draw is indexed by seed, attempt and lane; pausing, seeking and restoring cannot depend on hidden prior draws. Playback advances 20 attempts/second; web reading has no time limit. Changing the observation mode or initial door clears incompatible counts. Relation edits and other reader choices survive scene navigation. Reset restores the declared initial example. Invalid external snapshots leave the reader and adapter unchanged.

Scene suggestions follow the authored observation process until the reader explicitly selects a process, seed or initial door, or has sampled trials. The persisted `processSelected` flag preserves those choices across navigation and snapshot restoration. Relation exploration is available in `question`, `pairs`, `functions`, `close`; the base Monty ledger supports `strategy`/`compare`, while the public Monty reader wraps it in the optional `experiment` scene; family exploration in `filter`, `child`. Other scenes use their original static figure and prose. The family opening remains static. The public Monty opening instead uses three selectable hidden doors through [monty-hall.ts](monty-hall.ts); it reveals neither a prize nor a theoretical answer. When a selected experiment differs from the authored example, or sampling has begun, scoped model-derived narration supplements the canonical long-form explanation and explicitly distinguishes the selected comparison. Unknown host/reporting policies are never silently assigned probabilities.

## Observation and animation

Monty fixes always-switch, a uniform prize prior, and uniform host tie-breaking. Informed hosts always reveal an unchosen goat; blind hosts open an unchosen door uniformly. Sampling starts from the prior; prize reveals are excluded from the goat-observation denominator. No condition is placed on a specific revealed door number. The family model declares independent, equiprobable boy/girl outcomes in birth order. Family filtering and uniformly selected-child observation have different likelihoods. Counts distinguish attempts, accepted observations and successes; plotted frequencies use the accepted denominator. Reference probabilities are exact model values, not sample averages or population estimates. Fluctuations need not shrink monotonically.

For the two probability topics, a semantic case ledger replaces the generic SVG tree. The family ledger retains the probability-column representation below. Monty Hall instead shows the same exact model as six equal probability units, two for each prize location: retained units are checked, excluded units crossed out, per-row reasons explain the host action, and the footer divides winning retained units by all retained units. Its initial/reset/staged state is unchanged. Informed opening retains 2/2/2 units; blind uniform opening retains 2/1/1 around the initial choice. These units are a calculation device, never a claim about six actual trials. Reader-facing Monty labels and added narration use cases and counting rather than likelihood or normalization vocabulary. Its fixed rows are BB/BG/GB/GG for the family model and prize doors 1/2/3 for Monty Hall. BB notation explicitly identifies first-child/second-child order. Each family row reports prior, likelihood of the declared report, retained mass (`prior × report likelihood`) and normalized row weight. “Report likelihood” means the probability of receiving the stated report assuming that row is the true case; it is not another prior. The ledger states the multiplication operation, then shows that summing retained masses makes the observation denominator, and finally divides the target retained mass by that denominator. The Monty header visibly names the initial chosen door, labels rows as prize locations and states uniform host tie-breaking. Its event is “any unchosen goat is opened,” never a numbered-door event. `montyObservation` remains canonical for initial choice 1; the adapter maps canonical prize 1 to the reader's chosen door and canonical prizes 2/3 to the other actual door identities before display.

Stages retain a meaningful derivation order: stage 0 names every fixed case and the event; stage 1 exposes priors (Monty: two initial units per row); stage 2 exposes likelihoods and retained masses (Monty: checked/crossed units and reasons); stage 3 exposes normalized row weights and the exact target fraction (Monty: switching outcomes and winning/retained unit counts). Normal scene entry and reset complete stage 3, so exact reasoning requires no extra unfold action. Explicit unfold playback and absolute seeking may show earlier stages. Binary relations keep their existing SVG rendering and stage behavior.

Empirical playback is a secondary native disclosure below the exact ledger. Opening it preserves the existing trial buttons, count and seed inputs, chart, live status and deterministic state behavior. Explicit count controls and the slider select an absolute prefix; play/pause animates its progression. Hidden documents and scene changes pause sampling and unfolding. Reduced motion unfolds without delay. Exact values remain model outputs, never sample estimates.

On entry to an explanatory experiment scene, the unedited ledger is already complete: exact cases, prior weights, observation likelihoods, retained masses and normalization must be inspectable before any random trials or extra unfold action. The opening probability questions remain static and do not reveal their later numerical result. Reset restores the current scene's authored observation process, zero trials and the complete stage-3 ledger. Explicit unfold playback and absolute seeking may still show earlier stages; saved stages and reader-selected processes remain restorable. This presentation default does not change the pure model's time-zero frame or its probability fixtures.

## Layout and evidence

In bounded readers, the lab and explanation share the available reader height. Chaptered long-form explanations instead retain the shared reader's normal document scrolling and full prose typography; the lab's compact caption and chrome rules must not shrink their worked reasoning. Verify document/internal extents, control access **and lab/narration overlap**, including 960×540 CSS pixels at DPR 2, desktop and narrow reading. Bounded sample count prevents unbounded calculation; clipping controls or hiding overflow is not a layout solution. Studio installs and builds these adapters explicitly in isolated worktrees. Tests, seeded experiment output and local previews do not establish independent source, rights or publication approval.

Above the 650px lab breakpoint, a long-form experiment stacks its control groups in document order instead of sharing a narrow grid track with the trial buttons. The procedure selector is content-sized, can wrap its label group, and must display the full chosen rule. The exact ledger takes its natural content height; the former 230px visual budget does not apply to long-form probability scenes. At 390px the table reflows into labelled case cards without horizontal page overflow. These rules preserve the distinction between family questioning, child selection and the two host policies rather than truncating the semantically decisive option text.

## Monty narrative wrapper

[monty-hall.ts](monty-hall.ts) retains the shared validated `LabState` and delegates the optional `experiment` to this lab. Its other available scenes use short door views, a three-result summary, connected probability trees or compact paired sampling; the full ledger remains in the optional experiment. The topic-owned [scenario helpers](../../../../content/statistics/monty-hall/scenarios.ts) derive deterministic illustrative and hypothetical cases. Opening prize depends on the seed, never on which door the reader clicks. `processSelected` indicates an explicit choice on the opening; continuing without one uses the labelled door-1 example. No extra persisted fields or migrations are introduced.

The wrapper uses the selected door throughout the mainline. Case scenes explicitly assume a prize location; they are not new observations of the opening round. The blind mainline fixes its own named protocol even if the optional experiment retains a different edited mode. Static protocol, transition, close and numbered-door scenes keep their owned figures. The numbered-door branch explicitly declares its separate fixed choice1 and does not overwrite the user's choice. The full ledger is optional because the mainline independently works every needed case. The topic README and scene plan own content/purpose/success criteria; source, learner and publication reviews remain separate.

The Monty wrapper also exposes paired repeated draws in the mainline `sampling` scene. Its controls reuse the same count, seed and choice as the optional full ledger and compare informed/blind summaries on identical indexed prize draws. The sample pool keeps excluded prize reveals visible; only retained goat observations form the success denominator. Manual batches add attempts up to MAX_TRIALS. Clear resets attempts only; new sample increments the recorded seed and clears attempts. Playback delegates to the shared clock and pauses on scene change or document hiding. Mainline possibility trees label both conditional opening chance and joint path mass; the informed tree has unequal leaf weights.
