The rows below preserve the original pre-correction audit. For `rational-numbers/transfer`, distinguish cross-product comparison from midpoint construction and make the arithmetic/order visible; the absence of a response widget alone is not blocking. This calibration does not clear missing reasoning, unreadable figures, source gaps or any other scene's findings.

- Original pre-correction scene verdicts: **19 blocking**, **30 revise**, **14 adequate**, **0 unverified**. These counts preserve the historical audit, not a current corrective verdict; apply the calibration above. “Adequate” meant a defensible representation for the proposed local purpose, not verified comprehension or source accuracy.
- The transfer scenes generally show the answer immediately and have no prediction, reveal, reasoning capture, or explanatory feedback. They therefore cannot evaluate changed-condition reasoning, even when their prose is correct.
- The rational-representations and rational-density proof sequences carry the heaviest formal load while relying on text/formula cards joined by neutral dots. - Only the complex rotation scene provides a substantive authored diagram; at 390×844 its labels and angle annotation become too small/overlapping. The other scoped scenes have no authored figures or meaningful content manipulation.
